Mathletics Assign & Review

Role: Product Designer 3P Learning 2 years
Overview

Assign & Review is the feature Mathletics teachers use most. I led a redesign that unified three disconnected assign workflows into one, used by teachers across 16,000+ schools.

The problem

Years of extension had left teachers with three separate ways to assign work — new courses, legacy courses, and challenges & assessments — each with its own interface and logic.

The work

Rebuilt around a Learn, Revise, Assess framework in language teachers already use, brought live results into the same view as assigning, and added groups, class-locking and quick navigation across classes.

Outcome

16,000+

schools using the redesigned workflow

3 to 1

assign workflows unified

Learn · Revise · Assess

new teaching framework
