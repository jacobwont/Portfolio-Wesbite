Mathletics New Courses

Role: Product Designer 3P Learning 2022 to 2024
Overview

I collaborated with the team on a new course structure designed to give students a more intentional learning path — one that clearly moved from understanding, to problem solving, to reasoning, and finally to confident application.

The problem

The legacy course model was fragmented and inconsistent. Students were navigating content without a clear teaching progression, and the experience didn't support sustained learning habits.

The work

Designed a more explicit teaching framework to structure each concept in a progressive way, while aligning the experience with how students actually learn and build mastery over time.

Outcome

Clearer

path from concept to mastery

Consistent

framework across the course experience

Built

to support long-term student engagement
