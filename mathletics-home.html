Mathletics for Home

Role: Product Designer 3P Learning 6 months
Overview

Mathletics for Home is the version parents buy and manage on behalf of their kids. I redesigned onboarding and built a parent dashboard to fix a sign-up flow that was losing people.

The problem

Sign-up took 15 steps and routed parents through external payment pages. Most who made it through had no idea how to get their child logged in.

The work

Cut sign-up to 6 steps with payment embedded directly in the page, added clear next-steps right after purchase, and built a parent dashboard with weekly progress and an upgrade path to annual.

Outcome

15 to 6

steps in the new sign-up flow

Embedded

payment, no more leaving the site

↑ Revenue

measurable increase on the B2C side
