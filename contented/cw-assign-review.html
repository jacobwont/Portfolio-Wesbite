Mathletics Assign & Review

mathletics.com · Product Design · 2 years

View Figma Design Watch Marketing Video Read Marketing Article

Mathletics is used by teachers across 16,000+ schools worldwide. Assign and Review is the feature they interact with most. This was the project to make it actually work for them.

The problem

Mathletics had been built and extended over many years, leaving teachers with three disconnected workflows for assigning work: new courses, legacy courses, and challenges and assessments. It wasn't just that they were in different places. Each had its own interface, its own logic, and its own interaction patterns. Teachers had to learn and re-learn different ways of doing essentially the same job.


Teachers are busy. They didn't want to learn another workflow. They wanted something that felt familiar, used terminology they already knew, and got them to the right place without the overhead of working out where that was.

The work

Unified interface built around familiar patterns. Led the redesign around a Learn, Revise, Assess framework — a clear structure that gave every part of the platform a distinct and immediately understood purpose, using language teachers already think in.

  • Learn — new interactive activities and challenges
  • Revise — revision tasks and Skill Quests once students have the foundations
  • Assess — tests and assessments with real-time progress data

Consolidated three legacy workflows into one interface without making teachers feel like they were starting from scratch.


Assign and review in one place. This became the single place where teachers manage and understand their class. Live results surface directly alongside assignments, eliminating the need to navigate to a separate area of the site just to review student work. Assign something, see how it's going, all in the same flow.


Class overview and student progress. Gave teachers a broader view of how their whole class is performing, with the ability to drill down into individual students. Progress indicators show at a glance where students are performing well and where they need support, without having to dig through separate reports.


Groups and differentiation. Teachers can create groups within their class to assign work quickly to different sets of students, making differentiation practical rather than time-consuming. Assignments can be targeted to the students who need them without touching the rest of the class.


Restrict access and focus. Added the ability to lock students into completing an assigned task before they can explore other areas of the platform. A simple but meaningful feature — it gave teachers control over where students' attention actually goes.


Cross-class management. Built quick navigation across multiple classes, so teachers could assign work to several classes in one session without having to repeat the same steps each time.

The impact

Assignment rates increased post-launch. Teachers gained meaningful control over student learning paths without having to navigate multiple disconnected parts of the platform.


The unified review experience reduced the time between assigning work and understanding its outcome, making the whole teaching loop faster and more visible.


The product now explains itself — resolving a communication problem that had previously required external documentation and support to patch.
