3P Learning's products — Reading Eggs, Writing Legends, Mathletics, and Mathseeds — are used by over 20 million students across 16,000+ schools worldwide. This project was about making sure the experience matched that scale.

The problem

Four strong products, four separate portals, four different ways of managing students. For teachers it meant constant context-switching. For administrators it was worse — every new school year brought the manual work of migrating students into new classes across multiple platforms, with no guarantee the data matched up. Secondary students moving across multiple subject classes made this even more complex. The fragmentation wasn't just an inconvenience. It was quietly driving churn.

The work

I was part of a small team that built the unified portal. My contributions spanned:


Unified portal for teachers and administrators. One login. Full visibility across all four products simultaneously. Teachers can assign and unassign work, manage classes, and monitor progress without leaving the platform.


New school year management. A tool that handles student transitions automatically, preserving historical data regardless of how students move between year levels or subject groups. A major reduction in manual overhead, especially for secondary schools where students move across many different classes.


Reporting and demonstrating value. Usage reporting built in from the ground up, giving school decision-makers clear visibility into how each product is being used across the school and making it easy to justify renewal. Data export was designed to be flexible enough to work with the many different ways school systems manage their student records.


Student matching. A reconciliation feature to handle name discrepancies between disparate systems, reducing the manual rework that had previously fallen on teachers.


Marketing site. I led the design and build of the 3 Essentials landing page, framing the bundle around the three pillars of student performance: reading, writing, and maths.

The impact

This was a commercial repositioning as much as a product project. The insight driving it: customers with two or more 3P Learning products were significantly more likely to buy a third and stayed with the business considerably longer.


The unified portal made that multi-product value tangible in the product itself rather than just in sales conversations. Administrators had a real reason to consolidate onto the platform. The reporting tools gave decision-makers something concrete to point to at renewal time, shifting the conversation from cost to demonstrated value.
