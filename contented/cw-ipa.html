Infrastructure Partnerships Australia

infrastructure.org.au · Website Design & Development · 1 year

View Live Site

The problem

IPA was producing world-class infrastructure intelligence: policy research, event content, data analysis. But they were distributing it through a static website with no login, no paywall, and no way to differentiate the member experience. There was no mechanism to drive retention or make membership feel valuable in the product itself.

The work

I designed and built the membership platform end to end. That meant architecting a gated content experience with login and paywall, and designing InfraFlix: a unified content hub pulling together articles, policy research, podcast episodes, live event streams, and on-demand recordings. I integrated interactive data charts to surface pipeline and investment data visually, and designed a clear information architecture across all content types so members could navigate research, tools, events, and resources from a single destination.

The impact

IPA moved from a passive publishing site to an active digital product with real retention mechanics. Members now have a clear reason to return, and the organisation has a platform that matches the quality of the work they produce.
