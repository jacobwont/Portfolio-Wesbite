Australian and New Zealand Infrastructure Pipeline (ANZIP)

infrastructurepipeline.org · Product Design · 1 year

View Live Site

ANZIP is the authoritative source of infrastructure pipeline data for Australia and New Zealand, jointly funded by government and private institutions. This is the product I designed to make that data actually useful.

The problem

A national infrastructure dataset covering hundreds of live projects existed, but it wasn't usable. The people who needed it most — investors, government agencies, industry professionals — had no purpose-built interface for searching, filtering, or analysing it in a way that reflected how they actually make decisions.


The deeper problem was trust. Infrastructure data changes constantly as projects move through their lifecycle, and without a clear signal that the data was current and accurate, the whole thing was worthless. People needed to see that updates were happening in real time before they would rely on it.

The work

Search and filtering. Designed ANZIP as a search-first product with powerful filtering across sector, location, status, and procurement approach. The goal was to get any user to the project or dataset they needed as quickly as possible.


Data visualisations. Built a suite of dynamic charts covering pipeline status, location distribution, expenditure forecasts, and labour demand. Each visualisation was designed to surface the constantly-updating data clearly, so users could immediately see that the information was live and reliable.


Infrastructure lifecycle tracking. Designed a system that tracks each asset as it moves through the full infrastructure lifecycle, from procurement through to build and delivery. This gave users a clear picture of where every project sits at any given moment and made the freshness of the data visible rather than assumed.


Map and project detail. Built out an interactive map view allowing users to navigate geographically and drill down into individual projects. Each project page provides a full breakdown including completion updates, funding details, and associated news.


Private finance analysis. Designed a dedicated module surfacing $119B+ in candidate projects, giving investors an immediate read on opportunity without having to manually filter through the full dataset.


Pipeline Report. A monthly report built into the platform, connecting ANZIP back to the core Infrastructure Partnerships Australia newsletter and creating an ecosystem that keeps members returning regularly.

The impact

ANZIP is now a jointly-funded initiative between Infrastructure Partnerships Australia and the Australian and New Zealand governments, used daily by industry, government, and investors across both countries.


The platform has become a genuine revenue asset for Infrastructure Partnerships Australia, driving membership acquisition and attention in a way the organisation's previous site never could.


By making the data's accuracy visible through consistent lifecycle tracking and regular updates, ANZIP shifted from being a dataset people were aware of to a product people depend on.
