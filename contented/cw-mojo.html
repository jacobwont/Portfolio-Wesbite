Mojo Crowe

mojocrowe.com · UX/UI Design — Website & Platform · 6 months

View Live Site

MOJO is a leadership development platform founded by Ben Crowe, trusted by elite athletes, world champions, and organisations worldwide. This was the project to expand what it could be.

The problem

MOJO had a strong individual product but no credible path to enterprise. The website and experience weren't built to communicate team-based onboarding, and the brand wasn't positioned to show up in B2B conversations. The individual offering was working. The growth opportunity beyond it wasn't being captured.

The work

B2B and B2C unified experience. Designed a single digital experience that serves both individual learners and enterprise teams without creating confusion between the two audiences. The challenge was making both feel like they belonged in the same product without diluting either.


Teams offering and onboarding. Extended and restructured the website to introduce a dedicated teams offering. Designed onboarding pathways tailored to different team cohorts: early, mid, and senior career, each with their own entry point and messaging. Organisations needed to be able to see immediately how MOJO would work at scale across their people.


Brand repositioning. Supported the broader repositioning that gave MOJO the visual and tonal credibility needed to go to market as an enterprise leadership platform. The consumer product had warmth and personality. The work was to carry that through into a context where organisational buyers also needed to feel confidence and trust.

The impact

Unlocked a new revenue channel that didn't exist before. MOJO can now pursue and close enterprise clients at scale, with organisation-wide rollouts across different team levels and delivery formats.


The repositioning gave the business a second commercial lane alongside the individual product, without compromising what made the original offering compelling.
