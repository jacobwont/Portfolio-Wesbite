About

I make things, and I help other people make things.

I work where design, education, and technology meet, and I have for most of my career. Today I am a design mentor at the Apple Developer Academy in Bali, where I architect and facilitate a year long challenge based learning arc for 220 learners from over 19 countries. Alongside that, I also take on freelance design and product work.

My path ran through film and motion, then a Master of Interaction Design, then a decade of UX and product work, most of it in education technology.
