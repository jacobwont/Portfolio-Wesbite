Atlas — Daily Geography Quiz

Role: Designer & Developer Independent project Live on the App Store
Overview

Atlas is a daily geography quiz app I designed and built myself, end to end, and shipped live on the App Store.

What it is

A short, daily geography challenge — one puzzle a day, easy to pick up, built to bring a little learning into a habit people already have.

Why I built it

I wanted to take an idea all the way from design to a shipped product entirely on my own — design, build, and App Store release.
