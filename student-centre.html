Mathletics Student Centre

Role: Product Designer 3P Learning 2022 to 2024
Overview

Mathletics is used by over 3.5 million students worldwide. I collaborated with the team as part of a two-year redesign of the Student Centre and new course structure — a more coherent teaching framework, a modernised interface, and a certificate system rebuilt from 3 to 40 — giving students one clearer place to work, track progress, and earn recognition.

The problem

Student retention was falling. The platform was visually dated, the course structure wasn't grounded in a coherent learning framework, and the engagement model wasn't designed to keep students coming back consistently.

The work

I collaborated on a two-year redesign spanning the full student-facing platform: a more explicit teaching framework, a new Student Centre interface, and a certificate system that rewarded consistent weekly learning instead of one-off peaks.

Outcome

increase in maths activity completion

3 to 40

certificates earned grew after launch
