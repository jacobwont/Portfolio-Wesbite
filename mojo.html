Mojo — Repositioning

Role: UX/UI Designer MOJO 6 months
Overview

MOJO is a leadership development platform founded by Ben Crowe, trusted by elite athletes and organisations worldwide. I helped reposition it from a consumer product into one that could also sell to enterprise teams.

The problem

MOJO had a strong individual product but no credible path into B2B — the website and experience weren't built to speak to organisations at all.

The work

Designed a single experience serving both individuals and enterprise teams, built a dedicated teams offering with onboarding tailored to different career stages, and supported the brand repositioning needed to be trusted in B2B conversations.

Outcome

New

revenue channel opened for enterprise clients

B2C + B2B

one experience serving individuals and teams

3

onboarding tracks by career stage
