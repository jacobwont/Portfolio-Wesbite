Jacob Laird

Hi, I'm Jacob Laird.
I'm a UX/UI design generalist who loves working across the product lifecycle from discovery to delivery - from conducting user research and wireframing designs to building beautiful interfaces and coding prototypes.

Currently, I’m tutoring Interaction Design at the University of Technology Sydney and working on digital products that visualise the Australian infrastructure pipeline for a Sydney based think tank.

With a background in filmmaking and motion design I understand both the storytelling behind digital products and the creative execution that makes them shine.

I believe that good design starts by really understanding what people need to build delightful experiences that solve their problems.
